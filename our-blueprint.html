Our Approach

Purpose at the foundation.

We’re building Herbig Group around a simple principle: a development should have a clear purpose for the people it serves. That principle guides our first project and our thinking about what comes next.

Principles

Four principles guide our work.

01

Understand the purpose

Start with the intended use of a place and the people it is meant to serve.

02

Align the people

Build relationships around clear responsibilities and shared expectations.

03

Keep the project grounded

Consider the practical requirements of the site, the facility and its intended use.

04

Think beyond completion

Look beyond opening day to the everyday use and care of the place being built.

In Practice

Our first project puts that purpose into practice.

Fortitude Arizona is under construction in the Phoenix Valley. It is the starting point for our development work and the foundation on which we intend to build.
