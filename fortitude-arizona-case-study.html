Project Overview

A wellness campus built to bridge rehabilitation and high-performance care.

The Fortitude Arizona Headquarters is a purpose-built wellness campus designed to integrate clinical diagnostics, physical therapy, and high-performance conditioning into a single operational environment. By organizing advanced recovery labs, coaching spaces, and multi-tenant diagnostic programs around a central anchor building, the project creates a clear patient journey from rehabilitation to preventative care.

The flagship Fortitude Arizona Headquarters was conceived to bridge the gap between high-performance athletic diagnostics and accessible clinical physical therapy. By integrating advanced technology into a single, cohesive facility, patients transition seamlessly from injury rehabilitation to active preventative conditioning.

Herbig Group spearheaded the strategic property acquisition and custom interior architecture to maximize multi-tenant diagnostic and recovery programs.
